# VOCABI — Learning engine spec

All engine code lives in `src/engine/`, is pure, and receives `now: Date`, `timeZone: string` and `rng: () => number` as inputs. Every numbered rule below needs at least one unit test. Heuristic constants are marked CALIBRATE: keep them in `src/engine/config.ts`, never inline.

## 1. Units of knowledge
- The unit is a **sense** (word + meaning), id like `abandon.v.01`. Two senses of one word are tracked separately.
- Each sense has knowledge **layers**:
  - `recognition` — choose the Hebrew meaning (MCQ, 4 options). Always required.
  - `context` — choose the word that completes an English sentence (MCQ, 4 options). Required when `sense.layers` includes it (default true for the AMIRNET track).
  - `production` — type the English word from the Hebrew meaning + a sentence with a blank. Required only when `sense.knowledge === "prod"`.
- One FSRS card per (senseId, layer), via `ts-fsrs`. Desired retention 0.90 (CALIBRATE). Parameters: use `ts-fsrs`'s built-in defaults via `generatorParameters({ request_retention: 0.90 })` rather than custom-optimized weights for Phase 0 (CALIBRATE; revisit after real usage data — see `docs/DECISIONS.md` #14).

## 2. Rating answers
| Outcome | FSRS rating |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong, or "I don't know" button | Again |
| Correct but slow (> `slowMs[layer]`, CALIBRATE: 8000 / 15000 / 20000) or correct with an accepted typo | Hard |
| Correct | Good |
`Easy` is not used in Phase 0. A self-mark "I know this" in the learn step never rates a card; it schedules a verification item in the same session. Every review writes a raw log entry with the rating and the raw outcome (`outcome`, `ms`, `typo`, `mode`); replay uses the stored rating, the raw fields exist to recalibrate `slowMs` (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #38). Implemented in `src/engine/fsrs.ts`.

## 3. Mastery
A sense is **mastered** iff every required layer has a card with at least one successful review AND predicted retrievability at the horizon ≥ 0.90, where horizon = exam date (if set and in the future) else `now + 30 days`.
Display states per sense: `new` → `learning` → `recognized` (recognition passed) → `mastered`. A lapse on any required layer drops the sense back to `learning`. Never show `mastered` for a sense that has only self-marks. A lapse is an Again on a layer that had succeeded before, and it lasts until that layer passes again; the exam horizon is the local start of the exam day, and "+30 days" is 30 local calendar days ahead (plus the time elapsed since today's local midnight) (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #36-37). Implemented in `src/engine/mastery.ts`.

## 4. Placement test
### Bank
- Bands are a **disjoint partition of lemmas** (not word forms), by frequency rank (CALIBRATE): B1 1-1000, B2 1001-2000, B3 2001-3000, B4 3001-5000, B5 5001-8000, ACAD = academic-list lemmas (NAWL, open license) with rank > 3000, which are removed from B4/B5. Every lemma is in exactly one band. `src/engine/config.ts` holds only the rank boundaries; band sizes (including ACAD) are computed at content-build time into `content/placement/bands.json` and passed to scoring as `size_b` — never hard-coded (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #31). Until M5 builds the real file, tests use `src/engine/fixtures/bands.json`.
- Per attempt: 5 real items per band (30) + 10 pseudowords + up to 4 verification MCQs ≈ 44 items, ~4-5 minutes.
- Pseudowords are pronounceable non-words (e.g. "brindolate"). `content:check` must prove none is a real word or inflection in the lexicon/word list.
### Flow
- Yes/no: "do you know this word?". Items interleaved; at least 4 pseudowords inside the first 20 items; bands roughly ascending. Implementation: real items in strictly ascending band blocks B1 → B5 → ACAD (ACAD ranks above 3000), 5 of the 10 pseudowords at random slots 2-20 and the rest at random later slots; the first item is always real (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #32).
- Early stop: if two consecutive bands have real-word yes-rate ≤ 0.20, skip the remaining higher bands (`truncated = true`) — but only after ≥ 4 pseudowords were answered. Checked after every answer against the last two *completed* bands, so it can also fire when the 4th pseudoword arrives later. It never fires once the last band is complete (no higher band is left to skip). Truncation also skips the remaining pseudowords: the test goes straight to verification (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #32).
- Verification: up to 4 MCQs drawn from real words answered "yes" in B3-B5/ACAD.
- State is persisted after every answer; reload resumes at the same item. No back navigation.
### Scoring
- `h_b` = yes-rate on real words in band b; `f` = yes-rate on pseudowords (global).
- `reliable = false` if `f ≥ 0.5`, or fewer than 4 pseudowords answered, or verification accuracy < 0.5 with ≥ 2 verification items.
- Corrected knowledge per band: `p_b = clamp((h_b − f) / (1 − f), 0, 1)`; if `f === 1`, set `p_b = 0` (no division by zero) and `reliable = false`.
- Estimate: `known = Σ p_b × size_b`.
- Interval: per band use smoothed `p̃ = (p_b·n_b + 1) / (n_b + 2)`, `var_b = size_b² · p̃(1 − p̃) / n_b`; `sd = √Σ var_b`; `[known − 1.645·sd, known + 1.645·sd]`, clamped to `[0, Σ size_b]`, low rounded down and high rounded up to the nearest 250, then clamped again (Σ size_b comes from data and need not be a multiple of 250). Skipped bands add 0 to `known` and `0.15 × size_b` to the high bound (CALIBRATE). `var_b` is computed only for bands that were fully answered (`n_b` is always exactly 5 for an answered band — bands are skipped wholesale by the early-stop rule, never partially, so `n_b` is never between 1 and 4). Skipped bands (`n_b = 0`) never enter the `var_b`/`sd` sum at all; they are handled exclusively by the separate `0.15 × size_b` high-bound addition above. This is a deliberate design, not a division-by-zero gap (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #12).
- Scoring runs only on a finished or early-stopped test (the engine throws otherwise), so every answered band has all its items and every unanswered band was skipped.
- Output: `{ low, high, perBand: [{ band, p }], reliable, truncated }`; `perBand` lists all 6 bands (skipped ones with `p = 0`). The UI shows a range and never a single number; `known` is available only through the separate `estimateKnown(perBand, sizes)` helper, used by tests (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #33).

## 5. Answer checking (production layer)
1. Normalize both sides: Unicode NFKC, trim, lowercase, collapse inner whitespace, unify apostrophes (’ → '), strip leading/trailing punctuation.
2. Accepted answers = `sense.answers` (lemma, listed inflections, US/UK variants, listed hyphen/space variants).
3. Exact match → correct.
4. If the input equals any OTHER lemma or listed form in the lexicon, or one of the sense's `nearWords` (real words one edit from an accepted answer, precomputed from the reference list — `docs/DECISIONS.md` #34) → wrong, and record a confusion pair (`target`, `typed`). No typo tolerance in this case (affect ≠ effect, quiet ≠ quite).
5. Otherwise, typo tolerance: Damerau-Levenshtein distance ≤ 1 if the target is ≥ 5 characters, else 0. Multi-word answers: at most one typo in total. Correct-with-typo → rated Hard, and the correct spelling is shown.
6. Empty input is not submittable; "I don't know" is a separate button (rated Again).

## 6. Distractors
- Recognition MCQ (engine-generated): candidates with the same part of speech, frequency band within ±1 (ACAD is adjacent to B4 and B5, `docs/DECISIONS.md` #27), not the same word family, not listed as a synonym, sampled uniformly at random from the combined eligible pool of the track's shipping senses and the authored distractor-only lexicon (verified glosses, never taught as senses); the lexicon fills shortages naturally, and a taught word appearing as a distractor is extra exposure (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #35). The 3 picks have mutually distinct glosses (#30). Their Hebrew glosses must share no normalized gloss with the target (normalize Hebrew: strip niqqud, maqaf → space, remove parenthetical text, split on `,` and `;`). Example of a collision that must be blocked: about/approximately both → "בערך". A shipping sense having fewer than 3 eligible distractors within ±1 band is a `content:check` **error**, not a warning (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #3) — this must be caught in content review, not at runtime. Bounded runtime fallback, which should never actually fire given the content-time error: widen the search to ±2 bands; if still short, serve the item as **practice-only** (does not count toward mastery or rate an FSRS card) and log an error. The engine is pure, so it returns `fallback: "practiceOnly"` and the caller logs. Never let a recognition MCQ with fewer than 4 options count toward mastery.
- Context MCQ: uses the 3 curated `clozeDistractors` from content (authored in the same inflection as the answer and verified not to fit). If missing, the sense is not eligible for the context layer — never improvise.
- Options are shuffled with the injected `rng`; the correct index must be uniformly distributed (test with a fixed seed over many draws).

## 7. Session builder
Inputs: `now`, `timeZone`, `minutesPerDay`, `examDate?`, card states, track sense order, placement result, `previousOnTrackStatus`, the senses introduced today, and a `seed` (instead of a live `rng`; see the plan bullet below). Implemented in `src/engine/session.ts` (builder + in-session flow), `src/engine/ontrack.ts` (status) and `src/engine/workload.ts` (shared costs).
- Time per item (CALIBRATE): learn 20s, recognition 8s, context 15s, production 20s. Budget = minutes × 60, hard cap 30 minutes — this cap is independent of the selected `minutesPerDay` (5/10/20): it is a ceiling on optional "extra practice" overflow sessions, not a contradiction of the smaller selectable values (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #13).
- Fill order: (1) due reviews, lowest retrievability first; (2) recent lapses; (3) new senses in track order. "Due" = due by the end of the local day; started senses missing a required layer's card get practice items for it before new senses; a layer is rated at most once per local day (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #44).
- New senses from bands with placement `p_b ≥ 0.8` go **verify-first**: a recognition MCQ before teaching. Correct → recognition rated Good, no teach card; wrong → normal teach flow.
- `unmasteredTarget` = the count of all verified, not-yet-mastered senses in the track's content set, **independent of placement** — placement never excludes a sense from the target, it only decides verify-first ordering above (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #1). Separately, wherever a *cost in time* is estimated for new senses (new-words-per-day budget consumption, on-track projection below), a sense in a band with `p_b ≥ 0.8` is costed at verify-first price (recognition time only, ~8s) rather than full teach+practice price, since it is expected to skip straight to verify-first.
- New words per day:
  - With exam date: `daysLeft` = local calendar days until the exam; `taperDays = 14` (CALIBRATE). If `daysLeft ≤ taperDays` → no new words unless nothing else is due. Else `newPerDay = ceil(unmasteredTarget / (daysLeft − taperDays))`, capped by the budget.
  - Without exam date: up to 40% of the budget for new words (CALIBRATE).
- On-track status (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #4): `neededMinutesPerDay = (newLearningCost + projectedReviewCost) / max(daysLeft − taperDays, 1)`, computed only when an exam date is set (no exam date → no on-track status is shown at all).
  - `newLearningCost` = sum over remaining `unmasteredTarget` senses of their teach+practice time (verify-first price for `p_b ≥ 0.8` bands, full per-layer time otherwise, using the §7 time constants).
  - `projectedReviewCost = senses × requiredLayers × reviewsPerLayer (CALIBRATE, default 4) × perLayerReviewTime` (an estimate of ongoing review load, using the same per-layer time constants).
  - Inside the taper window (`daysLeft ≤ taperDays`): skip the projection; "needs more" fires only if today's actual due-review time exceeds the budget.
  - The function is pure and takes `previousStatus` as an explicit input (not internal state) to implement hysteresis without hidden state: status flips to "needs more" only when `neededMinutesPerDay` exceeds 110% of `minutesPerDay`, and flips back to "on track" only when it drops below 95% — otherwise the previous status is kept. The caller persists the returned status in `ProgressStore` and passes it back in as `previousStatus` next time.
- Spacing (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #43): learn steps are grouped; a new sense is never tested right after its learn step. Its first practice item comes at least `relearnGap` (3, CALIBRATE) other items later, and its layers follow in order recognition → context → production with the same gap. When there are too few items, the gap is as large as the items allow and the order still holds. Trailing due reviews move into the new block as spacers. More generally, no two consecutive items belong to the same sense, for every item kind (reviews, missing-layer practice, relearning copies, checks and teach flows inserted mid-session), whenever another order exists; each sense keeps its own item order and the learn group stays together (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #49).
- In-session relearning (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #40): an item answered wrong (or "I don't know") is re-queued once, at least 3 items later (at the end if fewer remain), and copies may overflow the budget by max(50% of the budget, 2 minutes), never past the 30-minute cap (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #47). The copy is never rated and never re-queued again, so each layer's FSRS rating is recorded once per day.
- "I know this" (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #41): on a learn step it never rates a card. It drops that sense's pending practice items and schedules a recognition check at least 3 items later. The check behaves like verify-first: correct → recognition rated per §2 and the other layers follow as practice; wrong → rated Again and the normal teach flow is inserted (its recognition practice unrated, already rated today).
- Session plan (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #42): `buildSession` returns plain JSON, deterministic from its inputs + `seed`; each item's question rng is derived from `seed` and the item id. The caller persists the plan after every event, so a reload resumes the same plan; an event for anything but the current item is rejected; a plan from an earlier local day is stale and rebuilt.
- Extra practice (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #45): after "done for today" the learner may choose "learn N more new words" (normal units, rated, counted as introduced today, within the 30-minute cap per session; across the day new senses never exceed `dailyNewMax = max(2 × planned newPerDay, 20)` (CALIBRATE), and once it is reached the choice is not offered. `projectTomorrowReviews` returns the extra reviews tomorrow for the UI's "more words today = more reviews tomorrow", `docs/DECISIONS.md` #48) or "review weak words" (weakest started, unmastered cards, never rated).
- Edge cases: nothing due and nothing new → "you're done for today" + optional extra practice; exam date in the past → prompt to update it; the session survives reload mid-way; items answered offline are kept.

### Progress store contract
Card states, review logs, and the persisted on-track status are read from and written to a `ProgressStore` (Phase 0: local implementation; Phase 1: Supabase, per `CLAUDE.md`). Per `docs/DECISIONS.md` #8, this store must be migratable to a server without data loss **starting in Phase 0**, even though the migration itself is Phase 1 work: versioned schema, keyed only by stable sense ids (never by array index or any non-stable key); store raw review logs (rating, timestamp, layer, senseId), not only derived FSRS state, so mastery/FSRS can be recomputed if the algorithm changes; expose `exportAll()`/`importAll()` with a round-trip test (export then import reproduces the same state).

## 8. Days and streaks
- A day is a local calendar date in the user's time zone (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Never compute days as 24-hour multiples (DST).
- Streak +1 on the first completed session of a local day. Freeze rule (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #6): no ISO weeks (Israeli weeks start Sunday — ISO week numbering was the wrong frame and could grant two freezes back to back across a week boundary). Store `lastFreezeDayKey`; a missed day is covered by an automatic freeze only if no freeze was used in the previous 6 local days (a rolling 7-day window, not a calendar week). Two consecutive missed days always break the streak — the freeze never covers a second consecutive miss.
- Changing the device time zone must not double-count or break a streak (use the stored day keys). A session whose day key is earlier than the stored last-session key changes nothing (`docs/DECISIONS.md` #39). Implemented in `src/engine/days.ts` and `src/engine/streak.ts`; day keys are built from `formatToParts`, never from a locale string format.

## 9. Required tests (minimum)
Placement: all-yes (f = 1), all-no, f = 0.5 boundary, early stop with < 4 pseudowords, truncated bounds, rounding to 250, clamping at 0 and max.
Answers: affect/effect, adapt/adopt, color/colour, "well-known"/"well known", trailing period, curly apostrophe, 4-letter word with one typo (reject), 6-letter word with transposition (accept, Hard).
Distractors: gloss collision blocked, same-family blocked, uniform correct position over 10,000 seeded draws, widen-to-±2-bands fallback, practice-only fallback when still short (does not rate a card or count toward mastery).
Session: empty state, budget cap, verify-first path, exam today, exam in 10 days (taper), exam in the past, DST change week in Asia/Jerusalem, resume mid-session, `unmasteredTarget` cost-weighting for `p_b ≥ 0.8` bands, on-track hysteresis (flip to "needs more" above 110%, flip back below 95%, held steady in between — including a dedicated flapping test right around both thresholds), `ProgressStore.exportAll()`/`importAll()` round-trip (M6), exam today, no exam date, spacing (minimum gap, small sessions keep learn → recognition → context → production), relearning re-queue, "I know this" verification path, rating once per layer per day, practice-only never rates.
Mastery: self-mark only ≠ mastered; lapse demotes; horizon = exam date vs +30 days.
Streaks: automatic freeze covers a single missed day only when none was used in the prior 6 days; two consecutive missed days break the streak; misses spanning a Sun/Mon boundary do not grant two freezes.
